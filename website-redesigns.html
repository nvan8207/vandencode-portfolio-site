Service 02 / Existing Websites
WEBSITE
REDESIGNS

For established businesses whose website has fallen behind the business itself.

Keep what works. Fix what does not.

A redesign is not about changing colours for sport. It is an opportunity to reassess structure, usability, content and visual presentation so the website once again represents the business properly.

GOOD REASONS TO REDESIGN

  • The business has changed significantly since the site was built
  • The site is difficult to use on modern phones
  • Customers struggle to find important information
  • The visual identity no longer reflects the business
  • Adding or updating content has become unnecessarily painful

WHAT THE REDESIGN CAN COVER

  • Content and navigation restructuring
  • Visual redesign
  • Responsive and accessibility improvements
  • New pages and service areas
  • Forms, calls-to-action and customer pathways
  • Migration or rebuild where the existing foundation is no longer suitable

If the business has grown but the website has not, this is generally the service to begin with.
