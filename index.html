001About the Studio

SMALL STUDIO.
CLOSE ATTENTION.

VANDENCODE is an independent web studio focused on creating thoughtful, practical websites for small businesses, professionals and creatives.

We keep projects personal and considered. That means taking the time to understand how your business actually works, what your customers need, and what your website needs to do — rather than simply dropping your logo into a template and calling it a day.

VANDENCODE is currently founder-led by developer and designer Naomi VanDenBerghe. Clients work directly with the person designing and building their site, from the first conversation through to launch.
